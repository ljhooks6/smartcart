## Required evidence before any pilot approval

Record accept/revise/exclude and conditions separately for ingredient composition/aliases, source-exclusion rules, preparation/handling, practical feasibility, quantities/yields/time, dietary/nutritional scope, and UX claims. Absence of a detected conflict is not clearance. Unresolved findings exclude the affected recipe/restriction combination. Approval is revision-specific; changed recipe, component, catalog or rules require impact review.

Cooking protocol: test measured batches for 2 and 4 servings (and every larger household size before permitting it); record raw/cooked/drained states, exact product labels, cookware dimensions, prep/active/elapsed/cooling times, thermometer endpoints where applicable, final usable weights/portions and observations/photos. Do not recruit allergic people to taste unreviewed candidates. A home cooking trial does not substitute for qualified allergy review. See [all-candidate matrix](physical-cooking-tests.md).
