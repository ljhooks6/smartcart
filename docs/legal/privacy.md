# Privacy Policy update — counsel review draft

Version: 2026-10-12-draft-v2

REVIEW DRAFT — not activated; requires attorney review and separate rollout approval.

This proposed update supplements the existing beta privacy notice; it does not silently replace existing consent. Contact support@mealcaddie.app for privacy questions, withdrawal and deletion requests.

Dietary entries can disclose sensitive health or religious information. Allergy selections, preferences, intolerances, medically related notes and legacy dietary text are optional and distinct. Do not provide diagnoses, severity or more information than necessary. We do not infer allergy severity from a preference.

Current architecture: planning settings are retained in the browser; planning requests and results may be stored in Supabase generation jobs and saved menus. Dietary text is sent to the configured AI service as part of a requested generation; hosting and database providers process it to deliver the service. Recipe Vault and pantry data persist under existing owner access controls. This is not a promise that dietary data stays on the device.

Dietary information must not be used for marketing audiences or engagement telemetry. Optional minimal beta analytics and optional marketing consent remain separate from participation and feedback. Feedback does not require either. Service providers, exact data categories, lawful bases, international transfers and any model-provider retention/training settings require verification and counsel review.

Proposed beta retention: unselected applications 90 days after recruitment closes; selected applications, feedback and aggregate reporting 90 days after program end; withdrawn applications within seven days; raw engagement events 30 days; abuse counters 48 hours; minimal program audit history 180 days; controlled test email ledger 90 days after the test. Agreement evidence is proposed for account/participation life plus 90 days after verified deletion, unless a specific reviewed legal hold requires longer. Privacy-request evidence is proposed for 90 days after resolution. Generation-job and provider-log retention, backups and support inbox schedules need final approval; no new cleanup is activated by this draft.

Request review/export, correction or account deletion through /account/privacy or support@mealcaddie.app. The request page verifies identity and queues private review; it does not immediately delete data. Support confirms scope, any applicable rights/holds, secure export delivery and confirmation before an approved deletion. Withdrawal controls remain available separately. No automatic email or billing change follows a request. Restored backups must reconcile approved deletion records before service resumes. Applicable response deadlines, parental handling and health-privacy obligations require counsel review; we do not assert HIPAA coverage or exemption.

No new sensitive data should be added to logs or analytics. Reasonable access controls and token hashing reduce risk but cannot guarantee security. Counsel should review FDA dietary language and FTC health-app privacy guidance and determine which laws apply.
