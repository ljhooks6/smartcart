# Beta Participation Agreement — counsel review draft

Version: 2026-10-12-draft-v2

REVIEW DRAFT — not activated; requires attorney review and separate rollout approval.

This proposed agreement is coordinated with existing invitation-only beta participation. It does not retroactively replace the consent already recorded or alter existing participation timestamps, invitations or grants.

Anyone may apply, but selection is not guaranteed and application submission does not activate access. Selected participants sign in with the verified account matching their personal invitation, reopen it and explicitly accept. Invitations expire seven days after issuance and are single-use. Participation ordinarily lasts 30 days after activation, capped at December 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM America/Chicago (January 1, 2027 05:59 UTC). Temporary Plus is not stored account Plus, creates no subscription and requires no payment. Paid subscriptions have not launched.

Temporary access may expire, be revoked or be withdrawn without deleting the participant's MealCaddie account, saved content or legitimate paid access. Promotions preserve expiration unless a separately authorized extension is approved. Shared database privileges can affect feature access across preview and production.

Beta software can fail and AI recipes need independent dietary review. Feedback is optional and can be submitted without optional analytics or marketing consent. Do not include medical details, passwords or other people's private information in feedback.

Participation withdrawal, privacy requests and reporting concerns are available through the participant controls and beta@mealcaddie.app; privacy contact is support@mealcaddie.app. Retention follows the provisionally documented schedules subject to review.

Terms acceptance and dietary acknowledgment, if approved later, must be separate from optional marketing/analytics and require fresh affirmative acceptance. No existing acceptance is fabricated. This draft is not a liability release.
