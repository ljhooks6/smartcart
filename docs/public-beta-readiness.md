## Preservation policy
An additive protected ledger snapshots ALL existing verified stored Plus users as existing_plus_pending_review, regardless of subscription evidence. This is access preservation, not billing conversion or proof of payment. Current administrators and active, nonexpired participants are independently authorized. Future genuine Stripe active/trialing records with a nonexpired period and provider reference are defensively preserved, but none currently exist. Current profiles, stored plans and subscription records are not changed. Browser roles cannot create preservation records.

Other ordinary accounts require active participation under the proposed gate; a Free profile alone is insufficient. Expiration, withdrawal, revocation and the program boundary are checked using current database state. New Free accounts cannot self-assign Plus or beta eligibility. The owner has approved preserving the individually audited existing Free account with saved content pending administrator review. The UUID-bound operator manifest must be applied and verified before gate activation; it does not grant Plus or exempt new Free accounts. No automatic notice is sent. Existing Plus must never be silently denied merely because payment evidence is absent.
